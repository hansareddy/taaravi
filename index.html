The Taaravi Story

From the heart of Hanamakonda, Taaravi curates handloom sarees that honour India's finest weaving traditions. Each piece is handpicked for its artistry, authenticity, and the story woven into every thread.

We believe that a saree is more than fabric — it's heritage, elegance, and confidence wrapped into six yards of grace. From the shimmering Maheshwari to the regal Banarasi, every saree in our collection celebrates the hands that create them.
